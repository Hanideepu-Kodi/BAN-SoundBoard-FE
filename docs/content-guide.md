# Content & Voice Guide (YouTube-style)

## Voice Principles
- Confident and hype, not shouty.
- Gamer/creator native but professional.
- Action-led labels: clear about what happens next.

## Core Labels
- Primary nav: Home, Explore, Playlists, Profile, Create.
- Actions: Play, Save to playlist, Share, View creator, Copy link.
- Privacy: Public / Link-only / Private.
- Mobile topbar: "Search sounds, tags, creators" placeholder.

## Hero & Key Lines
- Tagline: "Your board, your broadcast, zero friction."
- Subhead: "Discover sounds, hit play, save to your board."
- Create CTA: "Create sound" or "Upload sound" (opens modal).

## Sound Card Microcopy
- Menu items: "Save to playlist", "Share", "View creator".
- Hover helper: "Click to play" or "Click to play (stacks)".
- Empty tags: "No tags yet".

## Creator Profile Copy
- Plays count label: "Total plays".
- Tabs/sections: "Sounds", "Playlists".
- Empty state: "No sounds yet. Follow this creator for updates."

## Playlist Copy
- Section label: "Playlist".
- Buttons: "Play all", "Save playlist", "Share playlist".
- Empty state: "No sounds in this playlist yet."

## Upload Modal Copy
- Header: "Upload sound".
- Helper: "Max 60s. WAV/MP3/AAC. Auto-level on upload."
- Success: "Upload complete. Ready to play."
- Errors: "Unsupported format. Use WAV/MP3/AAC."

## My Sounds Copy
- Actions: "Edit", "Delete", "Change privacy".
- Empty state: "No sounds yet. Upload your first one."
