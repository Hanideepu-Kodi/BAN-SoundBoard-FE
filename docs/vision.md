# Kodi-board Experience Vision

## North Star
- Build a creator-first sound platform that feels like YouTube for audio snippets: browse, play instantly, save, and share without friction.
- Tone: confident, energetic, and clear. Gamer and creator native without being cringe.
- Promise: "Discover sounds, hit play, save to your board."

## Audience
- Streamers and creators who need fast, reliable sound triggers while on-air.
- Community curators who remix and share collections.
- Sound designers who want distribution and feedback.

## Product Metaphor
- Sounds are the primary content (like videos).
- Creator profiles are channels.
- Playlists are boards/collections.
- Plays are the core social proof metric.

## Visual Direction
- Keep the premium neon + glass look, but apply a media-feed structure.
- Home is a dense grid of sound cards with clear creator attribution.
- Left sidebar navigation anchors the app (Home, Explore, My Sounds, Playlists, Saved, Create).
- Top search is always available; card actions appear on hover or via the three-dot menu.

## Typography
- Display: Space Grotesk 700/600 for headlines.
- Body/UI: Manrope 500/400 for legibility in dense lists.
- Mono: JetBrains Mono for file meta and timings.

## Layout & Motion
- Sound cards are the primary unit; clicking a card plays immediately.
- Hover reveals action affordances (Save to playlist, Share, View creator).
- Multi-play remains core: clicking multiple cards layers audio without stopping the previous sound.
- Page transitions stay minimal; focus on fast content access.

## Accessibility & Resilience
- Strong focus rings and high-contrast text over imagery.
- Clear action labels for keyboard and screen readers.
- Reduced motion mode disables background shimmer and card lift.

## Differentiators
- Instant play on card click with multi-play dock always available.
- Creator pages show plays and collections at a glance.
- Share-first flows: link-only playlists and shareable sound links.
